# Preregistration Amendment 08 — secondary-control channel scope

Protocol: `P586-VMS-PLANT-0.1-20260803`

This amendment was frozen before DINOv3 embedding or target similarity analysis.

Secondary-panel manuscripts admitted under Amendment 07 contribute to:

- ordinary whole plants;
- masked whole plants;
- strict above-ground crops;
- padded/context above-ground crops.

They use the identical frozen Plant Mask Painter model, prompt, quantisation, root-boundary rule and objective area gates. Every terminal mask failure is retained in the raw whole-plant channel and excluded from derived channels under Amendment 04.

Dedicated reproductive detection and visual QA remain limited to the core target/reference/control panel. Consequently, flower and fruit/seed-head analyses use only manuscripts with a fully repaired and QA-frozen reproductive ledger. Secondary controls are not represented as zero-flower manuscripts; they are absent from those variants.

This separation prevents the expanded manuscript panel from delaying the primary whole/above-ground tests while preserving an honest flower-channel sample definition.
